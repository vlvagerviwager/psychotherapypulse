Psychotherapy Pulse

About

Psychotherapy Pulse is an international newsletter that curates psychotherapy research, innovations, and clinical developments from around the world for psychotherapists, Counsellors, clinical psychologists, counselling psychologists, psychologists, general practitioners, mental health professionals, psychotherapy students, counselling students, psychology students, researchers, psychoanalysts, and clinicians. It saves therapists and clinicians time by eliminating the need to subscribe to multiple regional newsletters and forums to stay current with international advances in counselling and psychotherapy practice.
